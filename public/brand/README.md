# Brand Assets

Place your logo here as `logo.png`.

- Recommended size: 256×256 or larger (square)
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- The image will be displayed at:
  - 36×36 px in the sidebar
  - 56×56 px on the login page

If `logo.png` is missing, the app will fall back to the built-in green SVG signal-wave icon.
